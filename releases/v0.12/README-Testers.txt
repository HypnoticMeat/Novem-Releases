NOVEM DUNGEON PLAYER - playtest build, rules v0.12
=====================================

Thanks for testing! Novem is a sudoku variant set in a dungeon. This build contains 5 puzzles.

HOW TO START
1. Double-click NovemPlayer.exe.
   - Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because this program isn't signed.
     Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
2. A small black window opens, and the game opens in your web browser.
3. Keep the black window open while you play. Close it when you're done.

Your progress is saved in your browser automatically. Run the program again to pick up where you left off.

HOW TO PLAY
- Open the Rules tab first, starting with "Terms" (adjacent, within reach, in line).
- The Party tab shows your adventurers, their levels and what each one MUST do.
- Click or drag to select cells:
  - Type 1-9 to enter a digit.
  - Shift+digit adds a corner mark, Ctrl+digit adds a center mark.
- Flag cells with M (monster), T (treasure) or D (door). Press R and click rooms in order to mark the route.
- Press Check to see mistakes, or to confirm you've cleared the dungeon.

WHAT TO SEND BACK
After you clear a puzzle, rate how hard it felt and how fun it was, then click "Save rating".
When you're done, open the Log tab, click "Copy ratings as JSON" and paste the result into an email or message.
Notes on where you got stuck are very welcome too.
